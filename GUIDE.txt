=============================================================
             🎚️ FOCUSFADE — SMART AUDIO DIMMER
=============================================================
A modern, ultra-lightweight Windows desktop audio tool:
Automatically lowers background music (YouTube, YouTube Music,
Spotify, etc.) whenever priority apps make sound (video editor,
Discord voice calls, games, meetings, etc.).

-------------------------------------------------------------
KEY UPDATES & FEATURES:
-------------------------------------------------------------
1. Multi-Trigger Support:
   - Add as many priority trigger apps as you want!
   - Example: Add BOTH "AfterFX" (video editor) AND "Discord" (voice call).
   - If you play your edit timeline OR a friend speaks on Discord,
     the background music automatically dims!
2. Natural Terminology:
   - Clean, straightforward labels (No weird "ducking parameters" or "video editor only").
   - "PRIORITY APPS (Triggers)" & "FADE & VOLUME SETTINGS".
3. Minimalist Semi-Transparent PiP HUD:
   - Ultra-compact DAW volume track strip (~280x72 px).
   - 85% opacity (translucent glass) so you can see your timeline underneath.
   - Shows live audio track levels for active trigger & music.
4. 100% Native & Lightweight:
   - Only ~39 KB standalone executable.
   - Zero installation, no pip, no Python runtime.
   - Near 0% CPU & ~20 MB RAM.

-------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO USE:
-------------------------------------------------------------
1. In "PRIORITY APPS (Triggers)":
   - Select an app from the dropdown (or type its name, e.g. AfterFX, Discord).
   - Click "+ Add" to add it to your active list.
   - You can add both AfterFX and Discord!
2. In "BACKGROUND MUSIC (To Lower)":
   - Select your browser or music player (e.g. msedge, chrome, Spotify).
3. Set your preferred "Lowered Music Volume" (e.g. 15%).
4. Click "▶ START FOCUSFADE".
5. (Optional) Click "⧉ Volume Track PiP" to open the floating transparent overlay!
